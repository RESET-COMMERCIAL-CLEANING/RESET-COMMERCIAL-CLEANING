# RESET Commercial Cleaning - Pricing Guide & Logic

## Overview
This pricing guide establishes consistent, transparent pricing for commercial cleaning services based on industry standards and RESET's operational costs.

---

## 1. BASE RATES (Per Square Foot)

### By Property Type (Annual Price Tier)

| Property Type | Standard Rate (/sqft) | Tier 1 (Budget) | Tier 2 (Standard) | Tier 3 (Premium) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Office** | $0.15 - $0.25 | $0.12 | $0.18 | $0.25 |
| **Retail** | $0.18 - $0.28 | $0.15 | $0.22 | $0.30 |
| **Warehouse** | $0.08 - $0.15 | $0.08 | $0.12 | $0.15 |
| **Medical/Dental** | $0.25 - $0.40 | $0.25 | $0.32 | $0.40 |
| **Restaurant** | $0.20 - $0.35 | $0.18 | $0.26 | $0.35 |
| **School/Education** | $0.12 - $0.22 | $0.12 | $0.17 | $0.22 |
| **Other/Mixed** | $0.15 - $0.25 | $0.12 | $0.18 | $0.25 |

**Notes:**
- Rates are in AUD per square foot per year
- Base rates assume standard cleaning frequency (weekly)
- Tier 1: Budget-conscious, standard service
- Tier 2: Standard service with quality assurance
- Tier 3: Premium service with enhanced features

## 2. CLEANING TYPE MULTIPLIERS

Apply these multipliers to the base rate:

| Cleaning Type | Multiplier | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard Clean** | 1.0x | Regular maintenance cleaning (weekly/bi-weekly) |
| **Deep Clean** | 1.8x | Intensive cleaning, shampooing carpets, detailed surfaces |
| **Post-Construction** | 2.5x | Debris removal, dust, specialized cleanup |
| **Specialized (Medical)** | 2.2x | High-touch sanitization, compliance standards |
| **Eco-Friendly** | 1.15x | Green products, sustainable practices |
| **Window Cleaning** | +$0.08/sqft | Per occurrence, separate from base rate |
| **Carpet Cleaning** | +$0.12/sqft | Per occurrence if included |
| **One-Time Service** | 1.3x | Non-recurring projects |

---

## 3. FLOOR MULTIPLIERS

Apply based on number of floors (accounts for complexity, equipment, time):

| Number of Floors | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| 1 Floor | 1.0x |
| 2-3 Floors | 1.15x |
| 4-5 Floors | 1.30x |
| 6-10 Floors | 1.50x |
| 11+ Floors | 1.75x |

**Rationale:** Multiple floors require:
- More equipment movement
- Elevator coordination
- Longer dwell time
- More staff scheduling complexity

---

## 4. FREQUENCY DISCOUNTS

Applied to annual pricing based on cleaning frequency:

| Frequency | Discount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily** | -15% | High volume, predictable scheduling |
| **Twice Weekly** | -12% | Strong relationship, stable income |
| **Weekly** | -5% | Standard frequency, no discount |
| **Bi-Weekly** | 0% | Baseline (no discount/premium) |
| **Monthly** | +10% | Less efficient per-visit cleaning |
| **One-Time** | +25% | Highest cost per visit, scheduling overhead |

## 10. PRICING CALCULATION FORMULA

### Monthly Service Pricing:

```
Monthly Price = [
  (Square Feet × Base Rate per Sqft) × Cleaning Type Multiplier × Floor Multiplier
  + Special Requirements Premiums
] × Frequency Multiplier
+ Distance Premium (if applicable)
- Volume Discount (if applicable)

Then divide by 12 to get monthly rate
```

### Annual Contract Pricing:

```
Annual Price = [
  (Square Feet × Base Rate per Sqft) × Cleaning Type Multiplier × Floor Multiplier
  + Special Requirements Premiums
] × Frequency Multiplier
+ Distance Premium (if applicable)
- Volume Discount (if applicable)
× Annual Contract Discount (if applicable)
```

### Hourly Rate (for custom work):

```
Hourly Rate = $45-65/hour based on:
- Tier 1 (Budget): $45/hour
- Tier 2 (Standard): $55/hour
- Tier 3 (Premium): $65/hour

Add premiums for:
- Specialized skills: +$10/hour
- Off-hours: +$15-20/hour
- Equipment operation: +$5-10/hour
```

## 11. EXAMPLE PRICING CALCULATIONS

### Example 1: Office Building (Tier 2, Standard)
- **Square Footage**: 10,000 sqft
- **Property Type**: Office
- **Cleaning Type**: Standard Clean
- **Floors**: 3
- **Frequency**: Weekly
- **Special Requirements**: None
- **Distance**: 15 km
- **Contract**: 12-month

**Calculation:**
- Base: 10,000 × $0.18 = $1,800/month
- Cleaning Type: $1,800 × 1.0 = $1,800
- Floor Multiplier: $1,800 × 1.15 = $2,070
- Frequency: $2,070 × 0.95 (weekly discount) = $1,966.50
- Distance Premium: $1,966.50 × 1.05 = $2,064.83
- Annual Contract Discount: $2,064.83 × 12 × 0.92 = **$22,796.58/year** or **$1,899.71/month**

---

### Example 2: Medical Facility (Tier 3, Deep Clean)
- **Square Footage**: 5,000 sqft
- **Property Type**: Medical
- **Cleaning Type**: Specialized (Medical) + High-Touch Sanitization
- **Floors**: 2
- **Frequency**: Twice Weekly
- **Special Requirements**: High-touch sanitization, after-hours
- **Distance**: 8 km
- **Contract**: 24-month

**Calculation:**
- Base: 5,000 × $0.40 = $2,000/month base
- Cleaning Type: $2,000 × 2.2 = $4,400
- Floor Multiplier: $4,400 × 1.15 = $5,060
- Frequency: $5,060 × 0.88 (twice-weekly) = $4,452.80
- Special Requirements: +$250 (sanitization) +$20/hour (assume 3 hours = $60) = $310
- Total per visit: ($4,452.80 + $310) / 8 visits per month = $595.98/visit
- Distance: No premium (under 10km)
- Annual: $595.98 × 26 visits/year = $15,495.48
- 24-month discount: $15,495.48 × 2 × 0.88 = **$27,272.04/year** or **$1,272.67/month**

---

### Example 3: Retail Store (Tier 1, One-Time Deep Clean)
- **Square Footage**: 3,000 sqft
- **Property Type**: Retail
- **Cleaning Type**: Deep Clean
- **Floors**: 1
- **Service**: One-Time
- **Special Requirements**: None
- **Distance**: 25 km

**Calculation:**
- Base: 3,000 × $0.15 = $450
- Cleaning Type: $450 × 1.8 = $810
- Floor Multiplier: $810 × 1.0 = $810
- One-Time Premium: $810 × 1.3 = $1,053
- Distance Premium: $1,053 × 1.10 = **$1,158.30** (one-time charge)
- Plus minimum if under $250: Already above minimum

- Pricing reviewed quarterly

## 13. NOTES

- **Inflation Adjustment**: Review and adjust rates annually (typically +3-5%)
- **Competitive Analysis**: Monitor local market rates quarterly
- **Cost Management**: Track labor, materials, and overhead to ensure profitability
- **Client Retention**: Use tiered pricing to attract different market segments
- **Minimum Viability**: Ensure prices cover:
  - Labor costs (50-55%)
  - Materials (15-20%)
  - Equipment/vehicle (10-15%)
  - Overhead/profit (15-20%)
